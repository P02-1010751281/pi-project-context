### Learning

- An occurrence count is not attribution. If a test name says “the shape line carries X”, assert on the shape line, not on the log’s total count.
- A shared sentence function does not cover its call sites. The command reply and the non-silent toast share `staleKeepSentence()`, but only the reply surface is exercised.
- Blocklist predicates need a documented scope plus positive fixtures; otherwise equivalent rewordings pass and unrelated phrases can false-positive.
- Mutation matrices rot when new assertions are added. Round 9 added several and the recorded counts were not re-derived.
